**Problem:** Bouquet of Flowers (Two Sum on Sorted Array)

**Key Learning (Two-Pointer Technique):**
When an array is already sorted and you need to find two numbers that sum to a specific target, use two pointers instead of nested loops. Start one pointer at the beginning (`left = 0`) and one at the end (`right = n - 1`). 

**Logic:**
* If `sum < target`, move `left` up to increase the sum.
* If `sum > target`, move `right` down to decrease the sum.
* This reduces the time complexity from O(N^2) down to a highly efficient O(N).
